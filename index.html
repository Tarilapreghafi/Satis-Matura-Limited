About Satis-Matura

More Than Enough.
Every Time.

Satis-Matura — meaning "enough and more" — is a Nigerian-owned logistics and supply company operating from Lagos. Founded on principles of timeliness and transparency, we serve shipping lines, oil sector operators, and government agencies with a reliable fleet and an experienced, locally-rooted team.

We know Lagos ports, depot procedures, and the unique demands of operating in Nigeria's most critical industries. That local intelligence, paired with rigorous operations, sets us apart.
